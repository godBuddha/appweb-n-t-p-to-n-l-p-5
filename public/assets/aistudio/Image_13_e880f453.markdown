## Brand & Style

This design system targets 5th-grade primary students (ages 10–11) mastering foundational math concepts—fractions, decimals, geometry, and measurement—alongside parents monitoring progress. The emotional tone must balance academic clarity with play-driven encouragement: focused, rewarding, frictionless, and cheerful without feeling infantile.

The design movement synthesizes modern soft-tactile UI and crisp educational minimalism:
- **Clean canvas, energetic accents**: Clean white and soft cloud backgrounds keep formulas and geometric figures legible, while vibrant gamified accents highlight achievements.
- **Friendly tactile affordances**: Layered buttons with bottom-edge borders evoke physical push-down clicks, rewarding completion of drills.
- **Visual learning first**: Abstract math operations are paired with high-contrast diagrams, color-coded fraction bars, and vibrant progress rings.

## Colors

- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Anchor color for primary actions, header navigation, active exercise states, and structural focus. Supported by a vivid sky tint (`#3B82F6` and `#DBEAFE`) for interactive surfaces and selected states.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: Gamification and motivational catalyst. Reserved strictly for streaks, star ratings, XP points, coin tallies, and unlock states. Paired with soft amber tints (`#FEF3C7`) for achievement badges.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Feedback and mastery color. Used for correct answers, 100% completion ribbons, and constructive verification tags.
- **Neutral (`#475569`)**: Balanced slate scale for optimal legibility. Neutral dark (`#0F172A`) drives primary question copy and math formulas; muted slate (`#64748B`) drives auxiliary subtext and unit indicators. Background sits on crisp porcelain (`#F8FAFC`) with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards.
- **Semantic Error (`#EF4444`)**: Used sparingly for incorrect choices, accompanied by explanatory tooltips rather than punitive visuals.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Nunito Sans** for prominent figures, headers, and UI anchors with **Quicksand** for instructional body content and math explanations. 

- **Readability & Numerals**: Ensure all numbers render with proportional lining numerals to maintain alignment across fraction bars, vertical long division, and measurement decimals.
- **Math Formulations (`math-display`)**: Applied to equations, fractions (numerator/denominator blocks), and unit conversions. Never set mathematical variables below 16px to prevent confusion between decimal points and multiplication signs.
- **Hierarchical Tone**: Headings convey friendliness via rounded terminals, while high weights (700 and 800) prevent visual weakness on smaller handheld displays.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first 4-column layout fluidly adapts to a 6-column grid on small tablets and an 8-column centralized container for standard tablets.

- **Canvas Gutters & Margins**: Default mobile screens maintain a strict `1rem` (16px) margin from canvas boundaries. Tablet screens scale outer margins to `2rem` (32px) to prevent overstretched question cards.
- **Touch-First Rhythm**: All tap targets (answer tiles, drag handles, numpad keys) must have a minimum bounding box of 48×48px with a minimum gap of `space-sm` (8px).
- **Vertical Scannability**: Exercise prompts use `space-md` separation between question stems, formulas, and multiple-choice grids to minimize cognitive overload.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical-tactile layering, crisp offset borders, and saturated ambient drop shadows rather than heavy generic blurs:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Screen canvases set to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**: Pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`) with a 2px solid border in `#E2E8F0` and an extra-soft ambient shadow (`0 4px 12px -2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.06)`).
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements)**: Gamified buttons and answer options use a 3D isometric base: a primary background matched with a 4px solid bottom inset line (e.g., `#1D4ED8` beneath `#2563EB`) that depresses by 2px on active press.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Victory Sheets)**: Gamification summaries and reward popups use an elevated shadow (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15)`) coupled with a gentle warm glow around gold badges.

## Shapes

The interface embraces pillowy, generous curvature to establish warmth and safety:
- **Standard Cards & Lesson Modules**: Utilize `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for clear separation and friendly borders.
- **Answer Selection Tiles & Input Fields**: Standardize on `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for finger-friendly comfort.
- **Action Buttons, Badges, & Chips**: Use full pill radii (`9999px`) to invite clicks and distinguish tags from informational cards.
- **Math Fraction Blocks**: Numerators and denominators sit within uniform rounded micro-cells (8px) separated by a clean horizontal bar.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Pill-shaped or rounded-2xl with `#2563EB` fill, white bold text, and a distinct `#1D4ED8` 4px tactile base shadow. On press, the button translates down 2px, reducing the shadow.
- **Secondary (Gamified Action)**: Golden Amber (`#F59E0B`) fill with `#B45309` tactile edge for daily challenges, bonus rounds, and double XP prompts.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Translucent sky wash (`#EFF6FF`) with blue text and a 1.5px `#BFDBFE` outline for back buttons and hint triggers.

### 2. Math Question Cards & Option Tiles
- **Multi-Choice Tiles**: Generous padding (`space-md`), white surface with 2px `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Selected State**: Light blue background (`#EFF6FF`), vibrant border (`#2563EB`), with a check icon badge.
- **Correct/Incorrect States**: Transform instantly into `#ECFDF5` with `#10B981` border (correct) or `#FEF2F2` with `#EF4444` border (incorrect), accompanied by an inline step-by-step hint.

### 3. Dynamic Progress Bars & Indicators
- **Lesson Progress**: Track height of 12px, background `#E2E8F0`, with a rounded fill of vibrant `#2563EB` containing a subtle animated striped sheen.
- **XP/Streak Bar**: Golden gradient fill (`#F59E0B` to `#FBBF24`) paired with an animated flame icon pill showing the day count.

### 4. Chips & Category Tags
- Pill-shaped tags (`label-md`) indicating math domains:
  - *Fractions*: Indigo tint (`#EEF2FF` text `#4338CA`)
  - *Decimals*: Cyan tint (`#ECFEFF` text `#0E7490`)
  - *Geometry*: Amber tint (`#FFFBEB` text `#B45309`)
  - *Measurement*: Emerald tint (`#ECFDF5` text `#047857`)

### 5. Input Fields & Numeric Pads
- **Math Inputs**: Clean white boxes with 2px borders, large centered numerals (`math-display`), accompanied by a built-in virtual math numpad featuring large circular keys (`48×48px` minimum) to simplify fraction and decimal entries without triggering full device keyboards.

### 6. Badges & Achievement Cards
- Circular or shield-shaped frames with dual-layered rings, glowing star accents, and bold contrast titles to celebrate milestones (e.g., "Bậc Thầy Phân Số", "Thợ Săn Điểm 10").
